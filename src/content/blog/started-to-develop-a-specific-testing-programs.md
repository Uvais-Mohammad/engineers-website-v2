Testing programs reduce ambiguity across the final phase of delivery. When inspections, approvals, and performance checks are defined early, site teams can sequence work without avoidable rework.

The strongest programs are specific to system type, operating conditions, and owner expectations. Generic checklists help, but project-specific criteria are what prevent handover surprises.

Commissioning should not be treated as a last-week activity. Teams that stage verification throughout construction gain faster defect resolution and better documentation at turnover.
