Projects usually slow down near completion because coordination complexity increases while visible progress appears to shrink. That is normal, but it needs active management.

Break the closeout stage into smaller measurable packages. Teams respond better to concrete punch-list progress, approvals, and handover checkpoints than vague pressure to “finish faster.”

Momentum is sustained when leadership removes blockers quickly, keeps trade sequencing realistic, and makes quality expectations explicit. Predictability is more motivating than urgency alone.
